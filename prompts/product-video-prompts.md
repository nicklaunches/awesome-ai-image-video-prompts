[First frame C (Shot 3)]
The same all-black chunky sneaker on the same black velvet with the same cold-white top-light system, restaged at a 45-degree overhead-side angle: the shoe occupying the right two-thirds, a large area of black negative space on the left, 16:9, no text.

[Shot 1 motion layer (I2V, first frame A)]
Start from the first frame, inherit its content and lighting; motion only. No text. Camera: one slow constant push-in, subtle handheld breathing, no cuts. Audio: a low drone, one muffled kick at 2s, faint velvet rustle.

[Shot 2 motion layer (I2V, first frame B)]

Start from the first frame, inherit its content and lighting; motion only. Action: [0s-1.5s] One highlight sweeps across the tread while the giant dark-gray background type "BUILT IN THE DARK" slides very slowly right to left, always behind the shoe, defocused and low-luminance; [1.5s-3.5s] The shoe flips slowly along a diagonal from sole view toward its side profile, briefly occluding more of the background type mid-flip, creating a foreground-silhouette versus background-type interplay; [3.5s-5s] It settles into a breathing hover as the background type slides off the left frame edge, cropping out and fading. Camera: locked macro, no cuts. Audio: the drone continues, one woody click at 1.5s, subtle fabric friction during the flip. Background text rules: the type never crosses in front of the shoe, never covers any part of it, stays defocused and low-contrast throughout, existing purely as a depth layer.

[Shot 3 motion layer (I2V, first frame C)]
Start from the first frame, inherit its content and lighting; motion only. Action: [0s-1.5s] The shoe hovers with breathing motion as the rim light builds; [1.5s-2.5s] The first half of the copy fades into the left negative space; [2.5s-3.2s] The second half fades in while the first half shifts gently left within the same line; [3.2s-5s] The copy locks, the shoe settles, the frame holds. Camera: one slow push-in, no cuts. Audio: the drone peaks, one crisp pluck at 1.5s; at 4.5s everything stops leaving the pluck tail.

[Edit & finishing]
Three 5-second I2V segments, two hard cuts aligned to the kick ending Shot 1 and the pluck opening Shot 3; one unified cool dark LUT across the film, an optional light grain overlay to hide batch differences. The product stays fully black throughout; only the lighting and the shoe's motion change. Copy rhythm: Shot 1 no text, Shot 2 background defocused type only, Shot 3 the main copy; at most one readable text layer on screen at any moment.

[Negative list (all three segments)]
no product color change, no extra light sources, no readable text besides the Shot 3 copy line, background text in Shot 2 must never overlap or occlude the shoe, no two text rows, no cuts within any single segment, no mirror floor reflections, no dissolve transitions, no logo.
